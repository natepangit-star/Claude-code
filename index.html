Filming tips

  • Whole body in frame, head to feet, for the whole clip.
  • Jumps / dunks: camera on a tripod, side-on, with your feet and the floor visible for the whole jump. Use 30 fps or more (60 fps is better). For an approach, film the last 2–3 steps and the take-off.
  • Shooting: film from the side (shooting-hand side) for angles and arc. Film facing you to check elbow alignment.
  • Dribbling: film facing you, 5+ seconds of continuous dribbling.
  • Tripod or a steady phone at waist height, 3–6 m away.
  • Good lighting, one player in frame, and try to film from the same spot each time.
